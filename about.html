Company History
OUR JOURNEY
IN INDONESIA
1975
SpanSet International Founded

SpanSet Group established in Europe, beginning over 50 years of engineering high-strength lifting, lashing, and personal protection solutions from synthetic fibres.

1997
PT. SpanSet Indonesia Established

Founded as a domestic Indonesian company, bringing world-class SpanSet safety engineering to the Indonesian market for the first time. Initial focus on lifting and load control products.

2008
ISO 9001:2008 Certification

Certified by Lloyd's Register Quality Assurance under ISO 9001:2008, formalizing our commitment to quality management and continuous improvement across all operations.

2013
Becomes Foreign Investment Company

PT. SpanSet Indonesia transitions to a foreign-invested company, fully integrating into the international SpanSet Group structure with access to global R&D, manufacturing, and innovation networks.

2015
TNI AD Military Certification

Products awarded Certificate of Testing from Dinas Penelitian dan Pengembangan TNI AD — a testament to the reliability and strength of our equipment under the most demanding conditions.

Today
500,000+ Hours of Field Experience

Our specialists have accumulated over 500,000 hours of field experience across Indonesian industries. We continue to expand our training, inspection, and product portfolio for tomorrow's challenges.
